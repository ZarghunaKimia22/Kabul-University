About Kabul University

Kabul University is a public institution of higher education in Kabul, Afghanistan, dedicated to academic excellence, meaningful research, and public service.

Our Mission

To prepare capable graduates through quality education, research, and engagement with society.

Our Vision

To be a trusted center of learning and innovation that contributes to Afghanistan and the wider world.
